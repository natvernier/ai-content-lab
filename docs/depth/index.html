Depth patterns

Fourteen ways a flat page can feel deep, on the laptop and phone you already have.

Ordinary gestures only: scroll, swipe, tap, drag, pinch. Every pattern keeps the scroll 1:1, keeps its words as real text, and has a calm mode that replaces the travel with a crossfade. The demos are graybox on purpose: gray shapes stand in for the art, so you judge the motion and nothing else.
